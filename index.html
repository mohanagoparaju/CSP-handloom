❖ TRADITION & PURPOSE ❖

About Chenetha Design Mitra

Bridging millennia of indigenous handloom weaving heritage with modern generative AI technology for artisan empowerment.

🏛️

The Sacred Art of Handloom

Handloom weaving in Andhra Pradesh and South India represents more than textile production; it is living geometry and spiritual devotion. In weaving clusters such as Mangalagiri, Uppada, Pochampally, Venkatagiri, and Dharmavaram, master weavers interlaced natural silk and zari to tell stories of sacred gopurams, blooming lotuses, and celestial swans.

⚡

Challenges Faced by Weavers

Traditional weavers face severe systemic obstacles: manual jacquard graph drafting on paper can take weeks for a single novel motif; rural weavers remain disconnected from fast-evolving urban bridal trends; and intense competition from industrial powerlooms creates economic pressure, driving younger generations away from pit looms.

🤖

Purpose of This Project

Chenetha Design Mitra was conceived as an impactful College Community Service Project (CSP) prototype. Our mission is not to replace the weaver, but to provide them with a digital design co-pilot: an intelligent, voice-first design studio that turns spoken customer ideas directly into visual saree layouts and technical yarn specifications.

🎙️

How the Tool Empowers Users

Weavers and customers can simply speak in Telugu ('పెళ్లి కోసం కెంపు రంగు పట్టు చీర') or English. The engine synthesizes authentic color harmonies, calculates warp/weft counts, drafts reed/pick numbers, and displays single large saree simulations across Flat, Drape, and Zoom modes before a single silk thread is cut.
